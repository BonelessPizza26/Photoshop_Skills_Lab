Using filters well

  1. Select the layer you want to change (duplicate it first with Ctrl+J).
  2. Choose a filter from the Filter menu.
  3. Use the Preview tick-box to compare before/after.
  4. Click OK.
💡 Less is moreA subtle filter usually looks more professional. If people notice the filter before the picture, it is too strong.
⚠️ Greyed out?Filters don’t work on Type or Shape layers until you rasterise them or convert them to a Smart Object.

Smart Filters (non-destructive)

Right-click a layer › Convert to Smart Object, then apply your filter. It appears underneath the layer as a Smart Filter:

  • Double-click the filter name to change its settings any time.
  • Click its eye to turn it off.
  • It comes with a filter mask – paint black to remove the filter from parts of the image (e.g. blur the background but keep the person sharp).
  • Double-click the small sliders icon to change its blend mode and opacity.

Using Smart Filters shows you understand non-destructive workflow – a key skill at Level 2.
