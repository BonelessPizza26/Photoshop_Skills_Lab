Understanding Layers

Layers are like clear sheets of plastic stacked on top of each other. What’s on a top layer covers what’s underneath.

Try the Layers panel

Click 👁️ to hide/show. Click a layer to select it, then drag it on the picture to move it. Use ▲▼ to change the order and try the opacity and blend mode.

Layer basics

➕

New layer

Click the + square at the bottom of the Layers panel, or Shift+Ctrl+N.

📄

Duplicate

Ctrl+J copies the layer – always duplicate before a big edit!

✏️

Rename

Double-click the layer’s name. Good names = organised work (and better marks!).

🔒

Lock

The padlock stops you changing a layer by accident. The Background layer starts locked.

📁

Groups

Select several layers and press Ctrl+G to put them in a folder.

🗑️

Delete

Drag the layer to the bin icon, or select it and press Delete.

🎭 Layer masks

A mask hides parts of a layer without deleting them. It’s a much better choice than the Eraser because you can always undo it.

Remember:⬛ Black conceals (hides)  ·  ⬜ White reveals (shows)
  1. Select the layer.
  2. Click the Add Layer Mask button (rectangle with a circle) at the bottom of the Layers panel.
  3. Pick the Brush (B) and paint with black to hide.
  4. Made a mistake? Press X to swap to white and paint it back.

Try it → the top layer is full colour, the bottom layer is black & white. Paint on the image to hide parts of the colour layer.

Grey on a mask partly hides – use a soft brush at low opacity, or a black-to-white gradient, for smooth blends between images. Alt+click the mask thumbnail to view the mask itself. Shift+click it to switch it off temporarily.

Types of layer

Hover the cards to flip them.

🟫

Pixel layer

A normal layer made of pixels – photos, brush strokes. You can paint and erase on it.
🅃

Type layer

Created by the Type tool. Shows a “T” thumbnail. The text stays editable.
⬟

Shape layer

Vector shapes from the Shape or Pen tool. Stays sharp at any size; change colour by double-clicking the thumbnail.
◐

Adjustment layer

Changes colour/light of everything below it, without changing those layers. Re-edit any time.
📦

Smart Object

A protected container. Resize without losing quality and use Smart Filters that can be edited later.
📎

Clipping mask

Alt+click between two layers – the top layer only shows inside the shape of the layer below. Great for putting a photo inside text!

Blend modes explained

Multiply – white disappears, colours get darker (good for shadows and line art). Screen – black disappears, colours get lighter (good for light effects, fire, double exposures). Overlay / Soft Light – boosts contrast; 50% grey becomes invisible. Colour – adds the colour of the top layer but keeps the light and shade of the layer below (used to colourise B&W photos).
